Privacy Policy – PLCForge

App: PLCForge: Automation Simulator
Publisher: PLCForge
Platforms: Android, iOS, Windows
Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

PLCForge: Automation Simulator ("the App") is an industrial automation simulation application for designing and testing PLC-based electrical circuits. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

2. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.

3. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Saved circuit projects
  • PLC ladder logic programs
  • Electrical panel and HMI layouts
  • Progress in tutorials, challenges and problems
  • App language and theme preferences
  • Premium purchase status

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any server of ours. A project file leaves your device only when you choose to export or share it yourself.

4. Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and does not use an advertising identifier.

5. Analytics

The App does not include any analytics or tracking service. We do not collect usage statistics from the App.

6. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time premium upgrade. The purchase is processed entirely by the store you downloaded the App from:

  • Google Play on Android
  • Apple App Store on iOS
  • Microsoft Store on Windows

Payment details are handled by the store under its own privacy policy. We never receive or store your payment card information. The store provides us with sales reports that do not identify you personally.

7. Third-Party Services

Apart from the store billing services listed in Section 6, the App does not integrate any third-party service that collects data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require any personal information to function.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: info@plcforge.net
