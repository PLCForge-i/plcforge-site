Privacy Policy – PLC Ladder Learn

App: PLC Ladder Learn
Publisher: PLCForge
Platforms: Android, iOS
Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

PLC Ladder Learn ("the App") is an educational application for learning Siemens S7 PLC Ladder Diagram programming. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

2. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as name, email address, or phone number. All learning progress is stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising

On Android, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect and use an advertising identifier (ad ID) from your device to show relevant ads. This data is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy. Premium users who have purchased the lifetime upgrade do not see advertisements.

On iOS, the App does not display advertisements and does not track you.

4. Analytics

On Android, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics that help us understand how the App is used and improve it. This includes:

  • App interaction events (e.g. a lesson was completed, a quiz was passed, the simulator was run)
  • General device information (device model, operating system version, country, app version)
  • A randomly generated app-instance identifier

Analytics does not collect your name, email address, or any account information, and the App has explicitly disabled the use of the advertising identifier (ad ID) for analytics. Usage data is not linked to your identity. Analytics data is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the App does not include any analytics service and collects no data.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time lifetime premium purchase. The purchase is processed entirely by the store you downloaded the App from: Google Play on Android and the Apple App Store on iOS. Payment details are handled by the store under its own privacy policy. We never receive or store your payment card information.

6. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Lesson and exercise progress
  • Experience points (XP) and level
  • Daily streak information
  • App language preference
  • Premium purchase status

This data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to any server. Separately from this locally stored data, on Android anonymous usage events are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase as described in Section 4.

7. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – advertising (Android only)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – anonymous usage statistics (Android only)
  • Google Play Billing – in-app purchases on Android
  • Apple App Store – in-app purchases on iOS

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require any personal information to function.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: info@plcforge.net
